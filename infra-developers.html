QUALITY FRAMEWORK

Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) Roadmap

Every stage of construction is governed by rigorous, ISO 9001:2015 audited procedural standards.

01
Phase 01
Geotech & Soil Analysis

Detailed borehole testing, bearing capacity assessment, and hydrological reviews before foundation layout.

  • Standard Penetration Tests (SPT)
  • Hydro-Geological Soil Profile
  • Foundation Load Analysis
02
Phase 02
Raw Material Testing

Batch-wise testing of cement, steel (Fe 550D), aggregates, and admixtures in certified testing laboratories.

  • Fe 550D Steel Tensile Tests
  • 53-Grade Cement Consistency
  • Aggregate Sieve Gradation
03
Phase 03
Precision RCC Pouring

Controlled slump tests, digital temperature monitoring, vibratory compaction, and continuous wet curing.

  • Controlled Slump Tests
  • Mechanical Needle Vibration
  • 28-Day Curing Regimes
04
Phase 04
Third-Party QA Audits

Non-destructive rebound hammer tests, ultrasonic pulse velocity checks, and final handover certification.

  • Rebound Hammer Testing
  • Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV)
  • ISO Zero-Defect Handover
