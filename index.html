Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries regarding Coastal Crest Group's corporate structure, civil engineering capacities, statutory compliance, and tender partnerships.

Coastal Crest Group operates across 5 key industry verticals:
  • Coastal Crest Infra & Developers: Highway engineering, asphalt paving, turnkey civil execution, high-rise residential apartments, and commercial parks.
  • Coastal Crest Ports & Shipping: Marine berthing structures, breakwaters, jetties, sea defenses, and automated container/bulk terminals.
  • Power & Infra: High-voltage transmission lines (AAA conductors), substations, and green energy grid infrastructure.
  • Hapliv Pharma India Pvt Ltd: Formulation manufacturing of Allopathy, Ayurvedic medicines, and cosmetics across WHO-GMP certified partner plants.
  • Pudami Consultancy Services India Pvt Ltd: Channel partner for premier real estate developers across PAN India, Dubai, and UAE.

Our registered corporate headquarters is located at Sai Srinivasa Apartment, Manikyanagar 4th Cross, Kanuru, Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh - 520007. We maintain regional branches and operational management nodes in Bangalore, Chennai, and Delhi, enabling seamless mobilization of heavy machinery, engineers, and project management across South and North India.

Coastal Crest maintains stringent statutory compliance and ISO quality benchmarks:
  • ISO 9001:2015: Certified Quality Management System covering civil, structural, and infrastructure execution.
  • DPIIT Recognition: Official Start Up India recognition by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade.
  • UDYAM MSME Registration: Registered under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Govt. of India.
  • Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA): Legally incorporated under the Companies Act with full statutory compliance.
