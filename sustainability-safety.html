SAFETY PROTOCOLS

The 6 Golden Rules of Site Safety

Strictly enforced across all project sites with zero exceptions. Safety is our non-negotiable licence to operate.

1. Mandatory 7-Point PPE

Hard hat with chin strap, high-vis reflective vest, steel-toe boots, safety goggles, cut-resistant gloves, ear protection & double harness.

2. Height Safety & Tie-Off

100% double lanyard tie-off required above 1.8 meters. Certified scaffolding, toe boards, safety netting, and daily tag inspections.

3. Trench Shoring & Slopes

All excavations exceeding 1.2m depth must be shored, stepped, or benched with safe ingress/egress ladders every 15 meters.

4. Plant & Machinery Zones

Segregated pedestrian walkways, 360-degree reverse alarm cameras, barricaded swing radiuses, and third-party certified crane operators.

5. Lock-Out / Tag-Out (LOTO)

Strict electrical isolation protocols with standardized padlock tagging during maintenance of power substations and panel boards.

6. Permit to Work (PTW)

Formal digital permit issuance for hot works, confined space entries, heavy tandem lifts, and high-voltage energization.

DAILY PROTOCOLS

Daily Site Safety Schedule

Structured 24-hour safety cycle executed at every active construction camp.

07:30 AM
Morning Tool-Box Talk (TBT)

Mandatory 15-minute briefing covering day's hazards, task risk assessments, and PPE checks for all workers.

08:30 AM
Scaffolding & Rigging Audit

Certified safety officers inspect heavy rigging tackles, scaffolding clamps, and crane limit switches.

01:00 PM
Mid-Day Hydration & Heat Check

Electrolyte stations, shaded rest sheds, and mandatory heat stress monitoring during peak afternoon sun.

05:30 PM
Daily Safety Log & Debrief

Near-miss reporting review, hazard rectification sign-offs, and digital HSE management reporting.
