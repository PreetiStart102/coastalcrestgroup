About Coastal Crest Group

Building Infrastructure, Shaping Future with Engineering Excellence

CORPORATE OVERVIEW

Civil Engineering & Multi-Sector Conglomerate

Founded in 2025, Coastal Crest Infra Developers (India) Pvt. Ltd. has rapidly established itself as a trusted, dynamic force in the civil engineering and construction industry. Headquartered in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, and operating pan-India with strategic branches in Bangalore, Chennai, and Delhi, we specialize in delivering end-to-end civil construction services.

We seamlessly manage projects ranging from boutique residential developments to large-scale public and private infrastructure projects. Driven by a team of highly qualified engineers and project managers, we combine cutting-edge technology and rigorous safety standards to deliver projects on time and within budget.

Our Vision

To be a world-class civil construction firm recognized for engineering excellence, sustainability, and transforming landscapes into landmarks.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, cost-effective infrastructure projects by employing innovative technologies, maintaining the highest standards of professionalism, and fostering a safe environment.
