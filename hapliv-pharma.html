PROCESS EXCELLENCE & CGMP COMPLIANCE

7-Step Precision Manufacturing Lifecycle

From quarantined raw material validation to final packaging, each stage operates under Master Formula Record (MFR) protocols and electronic batch documentation.

01
Dispensing & Sampling

Raw materials are quarantined in clean RM stores. Calibrated dispensing booths ensure precise micro-weighing under technical supervision.

02
Sifting & Blending

Powders pass through vibratory sifters and multi-mills. Homogeneous geometric dilution is achieved in Rapid Mixer Granulators (RMG) & cone blenders.

03
Fluid Bed Drying

Screened wet granules are dried in Fluid Bed Dryers (FBD) under strict temperatures not exceeding 55°C, followed by mesh screening and lubrication.

04
Rotary Compression

Quality-approved granules are fed to 27-station double rotary tablet presses. Line checks inspect weight variation, hardness, and thickness.

05
Auto-Coating Pan

Advanced autocoaters apply uniform film or enteric coatings to mask taste, enhance shelf-life stability, and regulate controlled gastro-intestinal release.

06
Blister & Alu-Alu Packing

Double track blister & Alu-Alu machines provide nitrogen flushing, tamper-proof sealing, batch coding, and cartooning at 7+ Lakh units per shift.

07
Dual QA/QC Testing

USP dissolution, friability, leak testing, and chromatographic assays are performed before final QA approval releases batches to Finished Goods (FG) store.

★
100% Traceability

Every batch follows Batch Manufacturing Records (BMR) with barcode verification for seamless recall safety and global export compliance.
